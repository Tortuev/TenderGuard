Experts’ Recommendations

Based on the three expert interview scenarios, several recommendations can be identified for the development of TenderGuard.

The tender specialist recommended automating the extraction of tender requirements and generating a compliance checklist. Each requirement should be linked to its original source in the tender documentation, and users should be able to review and correct AI-generated results.

The procurement and bid manager recommended implementing a centralized company document repository. The system should match tender requirements with relevant company documents, identify missing evidence, monitor document expiration dates, and protect confidential business information through access control.

The operations manager recommended simplifying complex tender requirements, clearly distinguishing missing, expired, and insufficient documents, and generating a final report showing the company's compliance status.

Based on these recommendations, the proposed first version of TenderGuard should focus on document upload, automated requirement extraction, requirement-to-document matching, evidence-based compliance classification, and human verification. Additional features, such as task assignment and advanced reporting, can be considered for future development.
