# Expert Interviews — TenderGuard


**Research objective:** To identify challenges in tender preparation and organization and determine how TenderGuard could help users manage tender requirements and documentation.

---

## Interview 1 — Tender Specialist

**Participant Name:** Murad
**Phone Number:** +7 747 791-32-75

**Q1. What are your main responsibilities when working with tenders?**

**A:** I review tender announcements, analyze requirements, prepare the necessary documents, and check whether the company meets the participation criteria.

**Q2. What is the most difficult part of preparing a tender application?**

**A:** Understanding all the requirements and making sure that every mandatory document is available. Sometimes the information is spread across several files.

**Q3. How do you currently analyze tender documentation?**

**A:** I read the documents manually, highlight important requirements, and create a checklist to track the necessary documents.

**Q4. How do you check whether your company meets a specific requirement?**

**A:** I compare the requirement with our company documents, such as certificates, licenses, contracts, and other supporting evidence.

**Q5. What mistakes can occur during this process?**

**A:** A requirement might be overlooked, a document might be outdated, or the submitted evidence might not fully satisfy the requirement.

**Q6. How could TenderGuard help you?**

**A:** It could automatically extract requirements from tender documents and compare them with our existing company documents.

**Q7. What should the system display for each requirement?**

**A:** It should show the requirement, its source in the tender document, the supporting company document, and the current compliance status.

**Q8. Would you trust an AI-generated compliance result?**

**A:** I would use it as a recommendation, but I would still verify important results manually. The system should explain why it assigned a particular status.

**Q9. Which feature would be most valuable to you?**

**A:** An automated checklist that identifies fulfilled requirements, missing documents, and requirements that need additional review.

**Q10. What would you improve in the proposed system?**

**A:** I would add clear warnings for mandatory requirements and allow users to correct AI mistakes.

## Interview 2 — Tender Specialist

**Participant Name:** Rashid
**Phone Number:** +7 777 241 3057

**Q1. What tasks do you perform when preparing tender applications?**

**A:** I review tender conditions, collect company documents, check qualification criteria, and ensure that the application is prepared according to the stated requirements.

**Q2. What problems do you encounter when working with company documents?**

**A:** Documents may be stored in different folders, and it can take time to find the correct version or determine whether a document is still valid.

**Q3. Which requirements are the most difficult to verify?**

**A:** Requirements related to previous experience, financial information, certificates, and specific qualifications can be difficult because the evidence may be contained in different documents.

**Q4. How do you identify missing documents?**

**A:** I compare the list of required documents with our available files and contact colleagues when something is missing.

**Q5. How much time does manual verification require?**

**A:** It depends on the complexity of the tender and the number of requirements. More complicated tenders require additional time for reviewing documents and checking evidence.

**Q6. Would automatic document matching be useful?**

**A:** Yes. It could suggest which company documents correspond to each requirement and help me identify missing evidence faster.

**Q7. What statuses should TenderGuard provide?**

**A:** I would suggest three main statuses: fulfilled, not fulfilled, and needs review. The last status is important when the system cannot confidently determine whether the evidence is sufficient.

**Q8. What security features would you expect?**

**A:** Company documents should be protected from unauthorized access. Each company should have access only to its own documents and results.

**Q9. What additional features would you like to see?**

**A:** Document expiration reminders, reusable company document storage, and the ability to export a compliance report would be useful.

**Q10. How would you evaluate whether TenderGuard is effective?**

**A:** I would compare the time spent checking tenders manually with the time spent using the system. I would also check whether the system identifies missing documents and helps reduce overlooked requirements.

## Interview 3 — Tender Process Organizer

**Participant Name:** Yergen
**Phone Number:** +7 775 000 5319

**Q1. What are your main responsibilities as an organizer?**

**A:** I coordinate the tender process, communicate with the people involved, organize documentation, and help ensure that the required steps are completed on time.

**Q2. What are the main organizational challenges?**

**A:** One challenge is coordinating information from different people and ensuring that all required documents and tasks are completed before the deadline.

**Q3. How do you track the progress of the process?**

**A:** I use lists, documents, messages, and direct communication with the people responsible for each task.

**Q4. What happens when a required document is missing?**

**A:** The responsible person must be contacted, and the document needs to be obtained or corrected. This can delay the preparation process.

**Q5. How could TenderGuard help with organization?**

**A:** It could provide a centralized overview of tender requirements, available documents, missing evidence, and the current status of each task.

**Q6. What information should an organizer see on the main dashboard?**

**A:** I would like to see the overall completion status, outstanding requirements, missing documents, approaching deadlines, and tasks that need attention.

**Q7. Should the system allow tasks to be assigned to different people?**

**A:** Yes. Assigning responsibility for missing documents would help the team coordinate its work and avoid confusion.

**Q8. Would notifications be useful?**

**A:** Yes. Notifications about approaching deadlines, missing documents, and expired certificates could help us resolve problems earlier.

**Q9. What would make the system easier to use?**

**A:** A simple interface, a clear checklist, understandable status labels, and a report that summarizes the current situation.

**Q10. What would you recommend for the first version of TenderGuard?**

**A:** I would prioritize document upload, automatic requirement extraction, compliance status tracking, and a dashboard showing what has been completed and what still needs attention. Task assignments and notifications could also be useful.

### Conclusion

The interviews suggest that TenderGuard should support both the technical analysis of tender requirements and the organizational work needed to prepare an application.

The two tender specialists emphasize accurate requirement extraction, document matching, compliance classification, and verification of supporting evidence. The organizer emphasizes task coordination, deadline tracking, and a clear overview of outstanding work.

Based on these preliminary insights, the first version should focus on extracting tender requirements, matching them with company documents, identifying missing or insufficient evidence, and providing a transparent compliance checklist. Organizational features such as task assignment and deadline notifications can complement these core functions.
